Key Features

9 powerful features that transform real estate transactions

01

AI Payment Scanner

Scan any payment plan with your phone camera. Our AI automatically extracts payment schedules, creates reminders, tracks deadlines, and sends smart alerts so you never miss a payment.

02

AI Property Assistant

Get personalized property recommendations based on your preferences, budget, and location. Our AI assistant compares deals, guides your decisions, and helps you find the perfect property without any middlemen.

03

AI Market Valuation

See the true market price instantly. Our AI-powered valuation engine analyzes real-time market data, comparable properties, and trends to give you accurate pricing—not developer assumptions.

04

24-Hour Instant Auctions

Buy or sell property in under one day. List, bid, buy, or sell through our instant auction system with full transparency. No waiting weeks or months—transactions happen in 24 hours.

05

Direct Developer Contact

Contact verified developers directly through our platform. No brokers, no middlemen, no hidden fees. Instant communication with developers who are ready to work with you.

06

KYC + E-Contracts

Fully digital, secure transactions from start to finish. Complete KYC verification and sign legally binding e-contracts from home. Everything is verified, encrypted, and compliant.

07

Portfolio Tracking

Monitor all your properties in one comprehensive dashboard. Track performance, value changes, risks, payment schedules, and property data in real-time. Get insights that help you make better decisions.

08

Fraud Detection

AI-powered fraud detection identifies fake listings, suspicious users, and price manipulation. Our system continuously monitors and protects you from scams, ensuring every transaction is safe.

09

Project Delay Predictor

Predict construction delays using AI analysis of historical and current project data. Know potential risks before you invest, helping you make informed decisions and avoid costly delays.
